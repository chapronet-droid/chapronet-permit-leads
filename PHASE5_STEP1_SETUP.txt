CHAPRONET PHASE 5 - STEP 1
LIVE COMPANY RESEARCH + LOCAL CACHE

WHAT THIS ADDS
- Research a permit owner, general contractor, architect, electrical contractor,
  expediter, or another company
- Uses OpenAI live web search
- Saves completed research in state\company_research.db
- Reuses saved profiles for 30 days by default
- Manual Refresh live research button
- Company identity confidence
- Website, public phone, headquarters, Chicago presence, industry, founding year
- Employee and revenue ranges when supported
- Decision-maker role suggestions
- Relevant projects
- ChaproNet sales strategy
- Recommended next actions
- Clickable source links
- Identity warnings and limitations

INSTALL
1. Close the dashboard browser tab and black Streamlit window.
2. Extract this ZIP directly into:
   ChaproNet_OS\Legacy working version\chapronet_permit_leads
3. Choose Replace files in destination when Windows asks.
4. Double-click install_phase5.bat.
5. Double-click run_dashboard.bat.
6. Open a permit.
7. Scroll below AI Opportunity Intelligence to Company Research.
8. Select the company role, verify/edit the company name, and click Research company.

ENVIRONMENT SETTINGS
These are optional because the system reuses your existing OpenAI settings:

OPENAI_RESEARCH_MODEL=gpt-4.1-mini
COMPANY_RESEARCH_CACHE_DAYS=30

BILLING
Live research uses the OpenAI API and web search tool. API charges are separate
from ChatGPT subscriptions. Saved local profiles avoid repeating the same research
on every dashboard load.

SECURITY
- Keep .env private.
- Do not commit .env to GitHub.
- Only public business information should be stored.
- Verify contact and company identity before outreach.

VERSION CONTROL

git add .
